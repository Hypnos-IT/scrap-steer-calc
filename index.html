Builder's Tips

  • Why Ackermann? During a turn, the inner wheel travels along a tighter circle (smaller radius) than the outer wheel. Setting a larger turn angle on the inner bearing eliminates tire dragging, stabilizes high-speed cornering, and prevents physics glitches.
  • Measuring in Scrap Mechanic: When placing blocks to measure Wheelbase (L) and Track Width (W), always subtract 1 block from your block count. Bearings pivot at their center, consuming half a block on each side.
  • Connecting to Driver's Seat: Steering bearings must be hooked up directly to a Level 5 Driver's Seat in game to customize turn angles and directions.
  • Integer Angle Approximation: Since driver's seats only take whole degrees, use the handy "approx. ~X°" values shown above in Blocks mode.
