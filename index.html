Builder's Tips

  • Ackermann what? During a turn, the inner wheel travels on a tighter arc (smaller radius) than the outer wheel. Ackermann geometry calculates a correspondingly larger steering angle for the inner wheel. This prevents the tires from slipping and the vehicle from "jerking", improving steering smoothness and traction.
  • How to measure in-game? When calculating the wheelbase (L) and track width (W) by placing blocks, remember to subtract 1 block from the result. Bearings rotate at their center, which "cuts off" half a block from both sides of the measured distance.
  • How to edit bearing turn angles? In the game it's only possible when the bearings are connected directly to the Level 5 driver's seat.
  • But I can't input exact angles in game! Just round the results from the calculator to the nearest integer value (e.g. 25° instead of 24.8°).
  • Is it all neccessary? Not really, but it's fun and makes the vehicle handle much better.
